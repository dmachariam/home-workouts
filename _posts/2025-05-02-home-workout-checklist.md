# Home Workout Routine for Busy Parents

This checklist provides a quick and effective home workout routine that busy parents can fit into their day. Each section includes warm-up, main exercises, and cool-down for a complete workout experience.

---

## Warm-Up (5-10 minutes)

- [ ] **Jumping Jacks** (2 minutes)
- [ ] **Arm Circles** (1 minute)  
  - **30 seconds forward**  
  - **30 seconds backward**  
- [ ] **Leg Swings** (1 minute)  
  - **30 seconds each leg**
- [ ] **Torso Twists** (1 minute)
- [ ] **Dynamic Stretching**:  
  - **High Knees** (1 minute)  
  - **Butt Kicks** (1 minute)  

---

## Main Exercises (20-30 minutes)

### 1. Bodyweight Squats
- [ ] **Sets:** 3  
- [ ] **Reps:** 12-15  
  - **Tips:** Keep feet shoulder-width apart, back straight, and lower until thighs are parallel to the ground.

### 2. Push-Ups
- [ ] **Sets:** 3  
- [ ] **Reps:** 10-12  
  - **Modification:** Knees on the ground for easier variation.
  - **Tips:** Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels (or knees).

### 3. Plank
- [ ] **Sets:** 3  
- [ ] **Duration:** 30-45 seconds  
  - **Modification:** Drop to knees for a modified plank.
  - **Tips:** Keep your core tight and back straight.

---

## Cool-Down (5-10 minutes)

- [ ] **Standing Forward Bend** (30 seconds)  
- [ ] **Child’s Pose** (30 seconds)  
- [ ] **Seated Hamstring Stretch** (30 seconds each leg)  
- [ ] **Cat-Cow Stretch** (1 minute)  
- [ ] **Deep Breathing** (2-3 minutes)  
  - Inhale through the nose and exhale through the mouth to relax.

---
